Privacy, Cookie & Visitor Telemetry Policy

1. Commitment to Ethical Transparency

Welcome to the professional portfolio of Hamim Mahamud Hamy (hamilio.netlify.app). We value your privacy and believe in radical transparency regarding how visitor telemetry is processed.

Key Takeaway: We do NOT sell data, do NOT use third-party advertising cookies, and do NOT track you across the web. You have total freedom to Accept or Reject telemetry at any time.

2. What Data We Collect

When you browse this portfolio with your consent, we record anonymous, non-personally identifiable metrics:

  • Approximate Geolocation: Country and city inferred from non-identifying IP lookup to understand international audience reach. We do not store your exact IP address.
  • Session Staying Time: Duration spent actively reading portfolio sections, case studies, and resume details.
  • Device & Browser Architecture: Device category (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet), operating system, browser engine, and screen resolution to optimize responsiveness.
  • Referral Channels: Traffic origin (e.g. LinkedIn, GitHub, Google, WhatsApp, Direct) to measure outreach effectiveness.
  • Direct Contact Submissions: If you intentionally fill out the contact form, your name, email, subject, and message are securely relayed to Hamim.

3. What We Strictly Do NOT Collect

  • No tracking across external websites or social profiles.
  • No personal identification numbers, financial info, or cookies sold to data brokers.
  • No marketing remarketing pixels or cross-domain ad profiling.

4. Cookies & Local Storage Usage

We use browser localStorage and sessionStorage solely for essential functionality:

  • Saving your consent choice (Accept or Reject) so you are not prompted repeatedly.
  • Persisting dynamic portfolio customization and real-time administrative edits.
  • Maintaining an active session duration timer while you browse.

5. Your Rights & Preference Control

You are in full control. If you choose Reject All, external IP queries and active duration telemetry are immediately disabled, and your choice is honored. You can reopen this policy and modify your decision at any time via the "Privacy & Policy" link in the site footer.
